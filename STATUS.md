# Status

Living checklist against the product in [`Make Email Great Again.md`](./Make%20Email%20Great%20Again.md). Update this when a surface ships or is cut.

## Shipped

**Shell.** Staff-off homepage: left rail (~220px), center feed, Co-Pilot drawer collapsed. Magazine | Raw. Paper stocks: cream, newsprint, sepia, night. Compose overlay (Cmd+N), with Focus for full-window writing. Settings overlay is mailboxes only — no separate Add Account window. Hire staff opens the BYOK modal. Keyboard: j/k, Enter, r, a (reply all), f (forward), c, e (archive), u (unread), s (flag), x (select), z (undo), Ctrl+S (save draft), Ctrl+K or `/` (palette), Esc. Overlay hover scrollbars on rail and feed. **N need you** under the mark is unread Action. **Awaiting a reply** is a badge for open loops (flagged to chase, or sent with no reply after four days) — not the staff drawer. **Uncertain** is a badge for weak classifier matches — they stay off Action until you place them. **Radar** is a badge for meeting invites that already arrived as mail — not a calendar. The rail only mentions sync when it is fetching or has failed — not while IMAP is quietly watching. The right rail is Hire staff until you hire; then it is **Next** when unread Action (or an awaiting loop) is waiting, otherwise Staff. Expanding Next shows that letter — Open, Reply, Draft a reply if drafts are on. The desk does not auto-open and never sends. No fake “Acme quote” copy. Fixture / demo mailboxes are gone; the UI never seeds sample accounts or messages. Magazine Action is a cover story, a briefing strip, and a Reading digest of article teasers. Same-subject threads stack as a developing story; with staff hired you can pin, rename, merge, or mark not a story, and the rail lists those stories. **Back issues** is archived mail as a monthly shelf, not a folder tree. Feeds list newest received first. Raw stays the dense from/subject/preview table. Empty Action is “Nothing needs you right now,” plus today’s receipt when you actually triaged. Click a sender for a page of their letters and send frequency. `x` or the checkbox multi-selects; then e / s / u apply to the set. Archive, flag, unread, and send undo from the toast or `z`. Archive and send wait eight seconds so undo is a restore, not a recall. **Clippings** is a badge for quotes you kept from letters — not a notebook. Ctrl+K searches the FTS5 index and runs commands (jump to feed/account/story, magazine/raw, paper stock, archive this feed or Reading, awaiting reply, Uncertain, Radar, clippings, back issues, reply all, forward this letter, staff desk).

**Reader.** Opens as a full-window overlay, not a shell column. The HTML MIME part is what we open, not the text conversion. Newsletters (Facebook, Gmail, etc.) keep `<style>` as CSS — including `:root` color-scheme, `@font-face`, and Gmail’s `u + .body` hacks — instead of dumping those rules as the letter. Scripts, forms, and `javascript:` URLs are stripped; tables, images, and author CSS stay. The iframe does not force Bateleur serif/charcoal onto the letter. Letters stay in a light color scheme so OS night mode does not paint newsletter chrome black; author layout CSS still applies. Click-drag does not rubber-band the letter; wheel and keys over the iframe scroll the overlay. The iframe stays `sandbox="allow-same-origin"` without `allow-scripts` — the browser may log that it blocked `about:srcdoc` script; that is the sandbox working. Remote http(s) images are blocked until you load them (this letter, or always in Settings). `cid:` inline images rewrite from cached MIME parts. http(s) links open in the system browser. `mailto:` opens Compose in Bateleur. Plain text autolinks URLs and addresses. Toggle to plain text when HTML is present. Gmail App-password hint in Settings. Opening a letter STOREs `\Seen`. Reader footer: Unread, Flag, Archive, Reply all (when there are extra recipients), Forward, Reply. Files list with Save to Downloads. A **Radar** card appears when the letter includes a calendar invite (when / where / organizer, Open in calendar). Select text in the letter (parent JS on the iframe `contentDocument`, no `allow-scripts`) and **Keep** it as a clipping. Kept quotes list under that letter and in Clippings. Magazine reader adds send frequency, a reading-time estimate, a pull-quote lede, more from this sender, and a numbered thread when three or more letters share a subject. Raw is the MIME escape hatch.

**Classification.** Action vs Reading is local heuristics (2FA, invoices, “please reply”, calendar invites, etc.). Weak matches (bare “invoice”, marketing invites, password tips) land in **Uncertain** instead of guessing Action. The matched signal is stored and shown as a category badge and **Why here?**. A `text/calendar` part lands in Action as Invite. Moving a sender to Reading twice pins them there; Guess again on their sender page clears that.

**Accounts.** Any IMAP or POP mailbox via Settings → Mail. Autoconfig guesses hosts for Gmail, Outlook, Fastmail, Yahoo, iCloud. Manual host / port / username. TLS via rustls (Mozilla roots + OS store), with an explicit “trust this certificate” override. Password or OAuth refresh token lives in the OS keychain (`bateleur.imap` / sanitized address), never SQLite. Windows Credential Manager caps one blob at 2560 characters; larger OAuth secrets are split across extra keychain entries, and the short-lived access token is not stored. Gmail and Outlook can **Sign in with Google / Microsoft** (OAuth 2.0 + PKCE, then IMAP/POP/SMTP XOAUTH2 — not the Gmail API or Microsoft Graph). That needs a Desktop / public-client ID once (Settings, or `BATELEUR_GOOGLE_OAUTH_CLIENT_ID` / `BATELEUR_MICROSOFT_OAUTH_CLIENT_ID`). Google Desktop apps also have a client secret (`BATELEUR_GOOGLE_OAUTH_CLIENT_SECRET`); the token host requires it. Microsoft must be a **Mobile and desktop** platform with redirect `http://localhost` (not Web / SPA) and public client flows on. App passwords still work. Save requires a persistent platform store (Windows Credential Manager / macOS Keychain / Secret Service) and round-trips through a *new* keyring entry so an in-memory mock cannot look like success. Re-adding the same address updates that mailbox instead of duplicating it. If Sync reports no password, add the mailbox again from Settings so the secret is written to the real store. **All mailboxes** is the combined feed (every account in Action, Reading, Sent, …); tap a mailbox to isolate it. Letters in the combined view name which mailbox they belong to.

**Sync.** IMAP LIST of folders, then fetch: last 40 INBOX, 40 Archive (skipped for Gmail All Mail), 30 each of Sent / Drafts / Junk, and up to eight custom folders (15 each). POP fetches the last 40 INBOX messages by UIDL and leaves them on the server; there are no POP folders. MIME parse (`mail-parser`), Action vs Reading classify (local heuristics: 2FA, invoices, “please reply”, etc.), SQLite cache (`bateleur.db` in app data). Unified inbox or per-account. Sync reuses the keychain password. After connect, IMAP IDLEs INBOX (RFC 2177) and refetches on change or every 8 minutes; if the server refuses IDLE, it polls every 3 minutes. POP polls every 3 minutes. The rail shows Syncing / Sync failed only when those are happening; Watching and Synced stay off the brand stack. Settings → Sync still forces a fetch. After the first fetch, new unread inbox mail raises one OS toast (sender + subject, or a count). Settings **Notify when new mail arrives** turns that off. Boot and a manual Sync do not toast mail that was already on the server.

**Send.** Confirm-gated SMTP via `lettre` (STARTTLS on 587, implicit TLS on 465). Compose / Reply / Reply all / Forward / mailto: pick a From mailbox, then **Send** then **Confirm send**. **Save draft** (Compose or Ctrl+S) APPENDs to Drafts on IMAP (`\Draft`) or keeps a local copy on POP; opening a draft resumes Compose. Saving again replaces that draft; sending it removes it from Drafts. Close still discards unless you save. Compose **Focus** is a full-window writing surface. **Cc** and **Bcc** are behind those labels until you need them (mailto `cc=` / `bcc=` fill them). Reply keeps the original as a collapsed magazine quote under the editor (appended on send, not dumped as `>` in the box). Reply all puts the correspondent on To and everyone else on Cc, except your addresses and no-reply. A reply sets `In-Reply-To` / `References` from the original Message-ID. Forward uses an empty To, a `Fwd:` subject, the same quote, and original non-inline files that fit the compose cap. Reply from Sent goes to the original recipients, not yourself. `::thanks` or `/followup` inserts a local snippet; add or remove them under Snippets in Compose. After confirm, an eight-second **Sending** toast can still undo — the letter has not gone out yet. Then the same OS-keychain password or OAuth token is used. After SMTP succeeds, IMAP accounts APPEND the RFC822 to Sent (`\Seen`). POP accounts keep a local Sent copy. Empty body or a missing To address is refused. Bcc is on the SMTP envelope only — it is not written into the Sent copy.

**Flags.** Unread (`\Seen`), flag (`\Flagged`), and archive. Archive uses IMAP MOVE when the server allows it, otherwise COPY + `\Deleted` + EXPUNGE. Destination is `\Archive`, a folder named Archive, or Gmail `[Gmail]/All Mail` (`\All`). Archived letters stay in **Back issues** (a local copy, plus the Archive folder when it is not All Mail). Local-only Sent or archive copies (`sent:…` / `archive:…`) cannot STORE — sync first. IMAP succeeds, then SQLite. POP flags and archive are local only; archived POP mail stays on the server so other clients still see it, and Bateleur will not re-download it. Flag and unread undo immediately; archive hides for eight seconds, then MOVEs.

**Attachments.** MIME parts are cached in SQLite (up to 12 MB each). The reader lists non-inline files; Save writes to Downloads. Compose Attach adds up to eight files (8 MB each) as a mixed MIME payload. Forward copies those original files into Compose when they fit the same cap. Inline `cid:` images are not listed as files; they render in the letter.

**Plain text and summaries.** Subjects, from-names, feed previews, and the plain-text body decode HTML entities (`&nbsp;`, `&#160;`, `&amp;`, …) and strip leftover tags (`<strong>`, `<b>`, `<bold>`, `<em>`, …). Full HTML still renders those tags (nonstandard `<bold>` is treated as `<strong>`). Cleanup runs on display for already-cached mail; **Sync** writes the cleaned preview/body into SQLite.

**Staff.** Hire staff stores the key in the OS keychain (`bateleur.staff`, separate from mail) and the provider, model, endpoint, and capability switches in SQLite. **Summarize this message** and **Generate drafts** run on demand from the reader when the switch is on and a key is present. **Summarize this account** writes a Morning Brief from unread Action (feed, desk, or Ctrl+K). The list is at most eight letters; a line drops once that letter is read, archived, or leaves Action. **Summarize all new mail** is opt-in batch-on-sync: after IDLE or Sync, new unread inbox letters can get a per-letter blurb (capped). **Triage this letter** can move a letter between Action and Reading; **Triage new mail** does the same for new unread inbox letters after sync, capped, off until that switch is on. Local 2FA / password / KYC is not batch-overridden. A sender pinned to Reading still wins. **Draft an RSVP** is opt-in on a Radar invite — it opens Compose and never sends. A draft opens in Compose — Send then Confirm still required. The letter body goes only to the chosen provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter, or a compatible `/v1/chat/completions` endpoint). If the key is missing or a call fails, mail still works and the reader shows why. Settings is mailboxes only. Stories (pin / rename / merge / not a story) are local subject grouping, not a model call; the rail lists them only after staff is hired. Radar is local ICS, not a model call.

## Pending — mail client

These are required before Bateleur is an Outlook-shaped client, not a reader.

- Second-account proof is already possible; keep unified vs per-account as the user’s choice

## Pending — staff (only after mail works)

- No auto-send; Awaiting reply is flagged mail plus a four-day unanswered-sent nudge — still never an auto follow-up
- No our-cloud proxy, no bundled inference, no Gmail API, no Microsoft Graph

## Pending — platform

- Signed installers (`.dmg`, `.msi`, AppImage/`.deb`) and auto-updater
- Mobile shell (`tauri android/ios init`); hard parts are background IMAP and push
- Calendar protocol (CalDAV / Google) — do not fake a full calendar from nothing; Radar is invites already in mail

## Deliberately out of scope

Hosts that only speak a proprietary mail API and not IMAP/POP. Fake Balance %. Auto-send. Selling tokens or proxying inference.
